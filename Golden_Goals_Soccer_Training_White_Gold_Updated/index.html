About the trainer

Meet Brendley

I’m Brendley, a 16 year old competitive soccer player for SDSC Surf GA. I have played soccer since I was 4 years old, and it has been a major part of my life ever since.

I play winger and forward and have experience in competitive club soccer, showcases, college camps, and high level training environments. I created Golden Goals Soccer Training to share what I have learned with younger players and help them build strong fundamentals early.

My sessions focus on confidence, technique, and lots of touches on the ball. Boys and girls ages 6 to 10 are welcome, including beginners, recreational players, and club players.

Training location

Conveniently located in the 92127 area.

Sessions are held at a public grass field in the the 92127 area. The exact field and meeting spot are sent to parents after registration, availability, and payment are confirmed.

Players should bring a soccer ball, water, shin guards, and cleats or athletic shoes. Please arrive about five minutes before the session begins.

Payment

Cash or electronic payment

Payment instructions are sent after registration. A spot is confirmed once payment is received.

Cancellations
